# Chirac Demo Script - 5-Minute Presentation

**Target Audience:** Chirac Mayor and Village Council
**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Objective:** Showcase SPV Treasure Map's value and secure €500/year contract

---

## Pre-Demo Setup (2 minutes before)

- [ ] Backend server running (`python main.py` in backend/)
- [ ] Frontend running (`npm run dev` in frontend/)
- [ ] Browser open to http://localhost:5173
- [ ] Have `scripts/launch_checklist.py` results ready
- [ ] Prepare screenshots folder

---

## Demo Flow (5 minutes)

### Opening (30 seconds)
"Good morning! I'm excited to show you how SPV Treasure Map can transform Chirac's incredible 2,500-year history into a tourism revenue generator. We've already loaded all your data - let me show you what we've built."

**Key Stats to Mention:**
- 123 historical conflicts mapped
- 19 points of interest documented
- 3 AI-generated identity themes
- Ready to launch today

---

**Navigate to:** http://localhost:5173

## Handling Objections

### "€500 is expensive"
"Let's look at ROI. If this brings just 50 extra visitors at €10 each in tourism spend, you've broken even. Most villages see 200+ engaged visitors in the first year."

### "We already have a website"
"This isn't a replacement - it's an experience. Your website has information. This lets visitors explore, discover, and engage with your history. Plus, the QR codes work on-site while they're walking around."

### "Who will manage this?"
"It's self-service. The dashboard is designed for non-technical users. Update POIs, generate QR codes, check stats - all point-and-click. We provide training and support."

### "What about other villages?"
"Chirac is our flagship. You'll be featured as the premier example. Other villages will see you as the leader."

## Emergency Troubleshooting

### Map not loading:
- Refresh page
- Check console for errors
- Fallback: Show screenshots

### Demo site down:
- Have screenshots ready
- Narrate the experience
- "This is why stability matters - we ensure 99.9% uptime"

### Data missing:
- Verify with launch checklist
- Re-run data import if needed
- Always run checklist before demo!

## Notes

- **Practice this 3 times** before the real demo
- **Time yourself** - keep to 5 minutes max
- **Prepare for technical questions** about data sources, AI, etc.
- **Know your limits** - don't promise features you haven't built
- **Focus on value, not technology** - they care about results, not React/FastAPI

**Good luck! You've built something amazing. Show them why it matters.**
